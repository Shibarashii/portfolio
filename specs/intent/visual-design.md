# Intent: Visual design

**Dropped 2026-11-22.** Seanrei restarted the visual look: too plain or gray, too technical or stiff, too long or text-heavy. Kept as a record only. Don't design or build from it.

Confirmed 2026-11-22. Project cards changed the same day: screenshots now lead (see [project-cards](../ideas/project-cards.md)).

- **Outcome:** a datasheet-style site, like a well-made technical manual: thin ruled lines, spec tables and numbered figures. It opens on a solid deep green title block, then switches to warm off-white with green-black text. Dark mode uses very dark green instead of black.
- **Readers:** any recruiter skimming for role, skills and projects, then the engineer or hiring manager who looks closer.
- **Why now:** the look has to be settled before Explorations, Foundations and the styling task (T8) can start.
- **Success:** a recruiter finds the role and best projects on the first screen or two, and someone can describe the site a day later as "the green one that looked like a technical manual". It isn't mistaken for a template or AI output.
- **Constraint:** personality goes into how content looks and never gets in the way of finding it. Every figure must be accurate to the real project, and Seanrei checks each one. Projects are full-width cards between thin rules, and real screenshots lead. A drawn figure is used only where a screenshot can't show the work (Nail Feature Classifier).
- **Out of scope:** gaming or pixel art, terminal styling, bright or neon green, gradients, frosted glass, pill badges, bento or three-column card grids, scroll fade-ins and animated figures. Motion is limited to hover and focus states.
- **Still open:** the exact green (starting point `#12392B`), the fonts, and the job title. Green and fonts get settled by comparing versions in Figma. The title is Seanrei's to write.
